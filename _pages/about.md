Welcome ! I am Quentin Bonassies, a young researcher which defended its PhD thesis  recently at the University of Toulouse, France. I am currently seeking for postdoctoral opportunities in the field of hydrology, hydrodynamic modeling, remote sensing, and data assimilation. 

My research focuses on the assimilation of remote sensing data for flood forecasting and hydrological modeling. I am particularly interested in leveraging satellite observations, such as those from the SWOT mission, to improve our understanding and prediction of flood events.
