I am a young researcher which defended its PhD thesis in April 2026 at CERFACS in Toulouse, France. I am currently seeking for postdoctoral opportunities in the field of hydrology, hydrodynamic modeling, remote sensing, and data assimilation.

I am a graduate engineer (in 2021) from the agronomy school of Toulouse (<b>[INP-AgroToulouse](https://www.ensat.fr/fr/index.html)</b>, ex. <b>INP-ENSAT</b>) with a specialization in water resources and hydrology. I completed my graduation internship for my engineering degree at <b>[Artelia](https://www.arteliagroup.com/fr/)</b>, where I had to rewrite a 1D-hydrosedimentary model in Python, it allowed me to gain experience in numerical modeling and data processing. After that, I worked for 1.5 year as an study engineer at Artelia, where I worked on hydrodynamic modeling of rivers, oceans, and coastal areas using the [OpenTelemac](https://opentelemac.org/fr) modeling suite. The modeling work had different purposes, with a focused on environmental impact studies and design of hydraulic structures.

I completed my <i>Ph.D. in hydrology and continental surfaces</i> (Oct. 2022 - April 2026) at CERFACS (Toulouse, France), and co-supervised between <b>[CNES](https://www.cnes.fr/)</b>, <b>[CLS](https://www.cls.fr/)</b> and <b>[CERFACS](https://www.cerfacs.fr/)</b>. It aimed at improving the understanding and prediction of flood events through the assimilation of remote sensing data for hydraulic and hydrological modeling for flood forecasting or reanalysis. During my doctoral research, I focused my work on the new <b>[Surface Water and Ocean Topography](https://swot.jpl.nasa.gov/)</b> mission (Ka-band radar interferometry with a low angle of incidence) to understand this new observation for the study of water on Earth, particularly with regard to flood monitoring. I did a 2-months internship during my PhD at the <b>[Jet Propulsion Laboratory](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/)</b> in Pasadena (California, USA), co-supervised by Dr. Cedric David (NASA JPL) and Dr. Hind Oubanas (UMR GEAU, INRAE Montpellier) to work on the RAPID model for hydrological modeling and data assimilation on the Garonne basin (France).

My research focuses on the assimilation of remote sensing data for flood forecasting and hydrological modeling. I am particularly interested in leveraging satellite observations, such as those from the SWOT mission, to improve our understanding and prediction of flood events, in global and regional contexts.

You can find more information on my research interests and publications on this site. If you have any questions or are interested in potential collaborations, please contact me at <a href="mailto:qbonassies@gmail.com">qbonassies@gmail.com</a>.
